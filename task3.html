Cristiano Ronaldo

The Legend of World Football

Cristiano Ronaldo playing for Portugal

Cristiano Ronaldo-is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward for and captains the Saudi Pro League club Al-Nassr and the Portugal national team.

Key Milestones:

  • 1985 - Born in Madeira, Portugal.
  • 2003 - Joined Manchester United.
  • 2008 - Won his first Champions League and Ballon d'Or.
  • 2009 - Transferred to Real Madrid.
  • 2016 - Won Euro 2016 with Portugal.
